Alignment and Safety Atlas

One map from Concrete Problems in AI Safety (2016) to the state of the art in late 2026. The top half is about the model: how we train it to behave, how that fails, and how we oversee it when it might not behave. The bottom half is about the system around the model: how attackers turn text into instructions, how production agents are wired, how we measure all of it, and the rules that govern it. Click any chip for the problem, what was new, a diagram, a worked example with real numbers, the maths and the sources. Every number carries its status: V checked in the primary source, S seen only in secondary sources, U from memory, D derived.

Three ways to read it
The model view. Concrete Problems → RLHF → reward over-optimisation → natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking → AI control → chain-of-thought monitoring. Pick the flow “Reward hacking, 2016 → 2026”.
The builder view. Building Effective Agents and its five patterns → indirect prompt injection → lethal trifecta and Rule of Two → design patterns and CaMeL → adaptive attacks. Pick “Securing agents by design”.
The governance view. NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act → frontier safety frameworks → UK AI Cyber Security Code → zero-trust controls for agents → threat-intelligence reports. Pick “From principles to controls”.
