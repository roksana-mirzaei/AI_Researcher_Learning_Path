**CATEGORY V**

**Multimodal Models & Vision-Language Systems** (7 papers)

*AI that sees, reads, and reasons across modalities. These papers chart the convergence from separate vision and language systems into unified multimodal architectures.*

## Papers

- [Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020)
- *Radford et al. (2021)* — ICML
- *Why it matters:* Connected images and text in a shared embedding space through contrastive learning, laying the foundation of modern multimodal AI.

I have CLIP explanation here: https://github.com/SiliconBrainCommunity/ML-code-walkthrough/tree/main/notebooks/CLIP

- [Visual Instruction Tuning (LLaVA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485)
- *Liu et al. (2023)* — NeurIPS
- *Why it matters:* Showed how to create strong multimodal models by instruction-tuning a language model on visual conversations.

- [Flamingo: A Visual Language Model for Few-Shot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14198)
- *Alayrac et al. (2022)* — NeurIPS
- *Why it matters:* Demonstrated few-shot multimodal learning by interleaving visual and textual tokens in a single architecture.

- [Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11805)
- *Google DeepMind (2023)* — arXiv
- *Why it matters:* Natively multimodal from pretraining, trained on interleaved text, image, audio, and video from the ground up.

- [Segment Anything (SAM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02643)
- *Kirillov et al. (2023)* — ICCV
- *Why it matters:* A foundation model for image segmentation that generalises zero-shot to any domain.

- [DINOv2: Learning Robust Visual Features without Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193)
- *Oquab et al. (2023)* — TMLR
- *Why it matters:* Self-supervised visual features that rival or exceed supervised ImageNet pretraining across diverse downstream tasks.

- [Sora / Video Generation Models as World Simulators](https://openai.com/index/video-generation-models-as-world-simulators/)
- *OpenAI (2024)* — OpenAI
- *Why it matters:* Positioned video generation as world simulation, not just content creation, framing diffusion models as implicit physics engines.
